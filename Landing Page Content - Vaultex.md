**Know Your Brand: Vaultex \| Since 2003**

**Over two decades of moving safety forward.**

Since its introduction in 2003, Vaultex has grown from its manufacturing base in India to supply industrial safety footwear to customers worldwide.

With a 70,000 sq. ft. production facility in Greater Noida, the brand brings together footwear design, prototype development, manufacturing, and quality inspection. From stitching the uppers to finishing and packaging, the brand produces in-house, allowing close oversight throughout the process.

Quality checks extend beyond the finished shoe. Vaultex monitors technical specifications through development and production, with batch testing at its in-house facility against applicable BIS and CE parameters. For dealers, this is an opportunity to explore a footwear brand backed by manufacturing experience, an established product range, and an international distributor network.
